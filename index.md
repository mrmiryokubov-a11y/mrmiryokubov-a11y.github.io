# innwood Sheets MCP

Private tool used only by its owner (Mirayubkhon Miryokubov) to let his own Claude assistant read and edit his own Google Sheets and Google Drive files. It is not offered to the public.

Contact: mr.miryokubov@gmail.com

## Privacy Policy

- The app accesses Google Sheets and Google Drive data only of the account owner who authorizes it, only on his own computer.
- Data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties or used for advertising.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
